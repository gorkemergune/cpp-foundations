Lab 20 merged two sorted lists. With k lists, comparing all k heads at every step costs O(k); a min-heap of the heads makes it O(log k). Relink the given nodes — do not allocate new ones (the test checks the addresses).

C12-B · Find Median from Data Stream (hard)

Numbers arrive one at a time; after each one the median must be available. Keep the smaller half in a max-heap and the larger half in a min-heap, with sizes equal or the lower one bigger by one: the median is on top.

Tests: add 1, 2 → 1.5; add 3 → 2; then 200 random streams compared after every number with a sorted copy.
