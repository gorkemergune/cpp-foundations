1. Problem statement

The archive asks the heap questions on a min-heap (every item ≤ its children) and 2017 Assignment 5 stores it 1-based: h[0] is unused, the root is h[1], the children of i are 2i and 2i+1, the parent is i/2. (Labwork 8 is 0-based like Lab 43: children 2i+1, 2i+2. Same algorithms, other formulas.)

AS5 Q4. The array 12 28 14 41 18 67 52 25 10 11 4 19 37 7 22 is (a) turned into a min-heap bottom-up, (b) its minimum is deleted, (c) 19 is inserted; (d) a heap of 9 items is given only by its preorder 21 19 24 18 62 10 46 25 18 — write down its array.
